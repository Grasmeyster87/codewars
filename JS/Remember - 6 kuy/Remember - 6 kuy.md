<div class="markdown prose max-w-none mb-8" id="description"><p>Write a function that takes a string and returns an array of the repeated characters (letters, numbers, whitespace) in the string.</p>
<p>If a charater is repeated more than once, only show it once in the result array.</p>
<p>Characters should be shown <strong>by the order of their first repetition</strong>. Note that this may be different from the order of first appearance of the character.</p>
<p>Characters are case sensitive.</p>
